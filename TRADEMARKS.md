The MIT license in [LICENSE](./LICENSE) covers the code in this repository. It does not cover the GeoThai name, the G-pin symbol, the wordmark or the files that show them. Those stay © 2026 Pongsakorn Thipayanate, all rights reserved.

These files hold the name or the logo:

- `src/assets/logo.svg`, `src/assets/symbol-black.svg`, `src/assets/symbol-white.svg`
- `public/favicon.svg`, `public/favicon.ico`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, `public/og.png`

## What you can do

- Write "GeoThai" to say where your data comes from, or to link to this site or its repositories.
- Show the logo next to a link to GeoThai, without changes.

## What needs written permission

- Use the name or the logo for your own site, app, package or fork.
- Change the logo, or use it in a way that says GeoThai made or supports your work.

If you fork this repository, replace these files and the name before you publish your fork.

The data has its own license, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The fonts and icons have theirs, listed under Stack in the [README](./README.md#stack).
